# Shootr Privacy Policy Draft

Shootr collects account details, contact information, booking details, location data required for shoots, uploaded media, payment status, and device notification tokens.

Shootr uses this data to create accounts, match clients with Shootrs, process bookings, deliver reels, send service notifications, prevent fraud, and operate admin support workflows.

Uploaded images and videos are stored with Cloudinary. Authentication, booking data, chat, notifications, and admin records are stored with Firebase. Payments are processed by Razorpay; Shootr should store payment IDs and statuses, not card or bank details.

Users can request account deletion from the app. Some transaction, support, and legal records may be retained when required for fraud prevention, tax, dispute, or compliance reasons.

Contact: support@shootr.app

This is a launch draft. Have counsel review it before publishing to the Play Store.
